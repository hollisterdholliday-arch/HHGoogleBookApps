# App ideas

Ideas we've talked about but not built. Each saved idea gets its own folder
with what we know so far. Nothing here is built until the owner says go.

## Saved

- [Teleprompter](teleprompter/README.md): script floating under your camera
  while you record.
- [Explainer Studio](explainer-studio/README.md): YouTube-explainer-style
  polish with full control over text, shapes and smooth animation, live and
  in an editor; plus micro-lesson mode and Lesson Packs.
- [Tool Shelf](tool-shelf/README.md): an AI hub for your HTML tools:
  connects Claude (HTML) and Gemini (video) so videos go into your tools;
  organize by course and unit, versions, checks, drag-to-publish links.

## Other ideas from the brainstorm (not chosen yet)

- **Teaching Tool Maker:** build interactive HTML tools (flashcards, sorting,
  matching, step-by-step reveals, quizzes) without coding; they drop into the
  Recorder's teaching-tool layer.
- **Whiteboard:** pen/touch canvas with graph paper and math tools, made to
  sit inside a Recorder scene.
- **Class Timer & Agenda:** floating countdown and lesson agenda for live
  online classes.
- **Name Picker & Groups:** fair random picker and group maker; roster stays
  on the computer.
- **Student Video Response:** a one-button version of the Recorder for
  students to record answers for Classroom.
- **Feedback Recorder:** mark up a student's PDF while talking; save a short
  video to return.
- **Lesson Planner:** units and lessons linking recordings, slides, tools and
  notes in a local folder.
